TravelPicsLog · Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains which data TravelPicsLog uses, where it goes and what you can do about it. In short: your photos, videos and trips stay on your phone. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics, and the developer has no server that receives your data.

Who is responsible

TravelPicsLog is made and published by Matteo Ciani (the developer), a private individual based in Italy, who is the data controller for the little data described here that reaches the developer. For any question about your data, write to travelpicslog.app@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your phone

  • The photos and videos you choose, with their date and the place where they were taken (the GPS position the camera writes in the file). The app reads them only to put them on the map and to make the video. They are never uploaded.
  • The GPX, KML and KMZ tracks you open and the map links you share with the app.
  • Your saved projects: the stops, their places, the settings of the video and a small picture of the map used as cover. They are saved in the private storage of the app.
  • Your settings, for example the language of the map.
  • Small sample pictures of the maps, shown in the windows where you choose a style.

This data is deleted when you delete a project, when you clear the data of the app or when you uninstall it.

The videos you export

The video is made on your phone and saved in your gallery (folder Movies/TravelPicsLog). It shows the map of the places of your trip and your photos. It is shared only if you share it yourself: before sharing it, check that it does not show places you want to keep private, such as your home.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos: to show your gallery and read the photos and videos you choose. On recent Android versions you can allow only some of them.
  • Location of the photos: to read the GPS position saved inside the photos and videos. The app never asks for the current position of your phone.
  • Internet: for the maps and the online services described below.

Online services the app uses

Some parts of the app ask online services for data. They receive only what is described here and, like any internet service, the IP address of your phone. Each service handles this data under its own privacy policy. Some of them may process it outside the European Union, for example in the United States, with the safeguards described in their policies.

Mapbox: maps, routes and place names

Mapbox Inc. (USA) provides the maps. When a map is shown, the app downloads the map of the area on the screen. When a leg follows the roads, the positions of its start and its end are sent to Mapbox to find the route. When you share a map link that contains only the name of a place, that name is sent to Mapbox to find its position. When you tap Use place name in the window of a stop, the position of that stop is sent to Mapbox to find its name.

The maps also send Mapbox anonymous data about their use (for example when a map is opened, the phone model and the Android version), which Mapbox uses to improve the maps. You can turn this off in Settings, with the switch Share anonymous map data. The app does not give Mapbox the position of your phone.

Privacy policy of Mapbox: https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy

Wikipedia: facts about the places

While the video is exported, the app shows facts and pictures about the places of the trip. To find them, it sends Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation, USA) the approximate position of up to 8 stops of the trip, with no other data about you.

Privacy policy of Wikimedia: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy

Google: map links, purchases and backup

  • When you share a Google Maps link with the app, the app opens that link on the servers of Google to read the places in it.
  • The app is downloaded from Google Play, and the purchase that unlocks the export is paid through Google Play. The developer receives only what Google gives to sellers (for example an order number, the date and the country) and never sees your card or payment details.
  • If you allowed it in the settings of your phone, Google may send the developer anonymous crash reports and statistics about the app.
  • If backup is on in the settings of your phone, Android may copy the data of the app (saved projects and settings) into the backup of your Google account, to put it back when you install the app on a new phone. You can turn this off in the settings of your phone.

Privacy policy of Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you write to us

When you use Report a problem or Suggest an idea, your email app sends the developer your email address, what you write and, at the end of the message, the version of the app, the phone model and the Android version (you can delete these lines before sending). They are used only to answer you and to improve the app, and are deleted when they are no longer needed, at the latest 2 years after the last message.

Why your data can be used

Under the GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679): the data used on your phone and the requests to the online services are needed to give you the features you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b)); the anonymous map data are based on the legitimate interest in improving the maps (Art. 6(1)(f)), and you can object at any time with the switch in Settings; your emails are used to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b)) and, for the ideas and problems you report, in the legitimate interest of improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f)).

The developer does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising and does not build profiles about you.

Your rights

You can ask the developer to see, correct or delete the data about you that the developer has (in practice, only the emails you sent), to limit its use or to object to it, by writing to travelpicslog.app@gmail.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (https://www.garanteprivacy.it), or the authority of the country where you live.

The data on your phone is always under your control: you can delete your projects or uninstall the app. For the data handled by Mapbox, Wikipedia or Google, you can exercise your rights directly with them, as explained in their policies.

Children

TravelPicsLog is not meant for children under 14. The developer does not knowingly receive data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on its web page, with a new date. Important changes will also be shown when you open the app.
